## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* Schedule a call that rings at an exact time, once or daily, with the app
  closed: an alarm-clock notification on the alarm stream, looping until
  answered or timed out.
* Full-screen call screen over the lock screen, with a default
  `AlarmCallScreen` or your own via `AlarmCallOverlay.screenBuilder`.
* Answer and dismiss from the notification or the call screen; a daily
  schedule survives both.
* Permission helpers for notifications, exact alarms and full-screen intents.
